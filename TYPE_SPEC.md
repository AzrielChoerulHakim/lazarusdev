# Atelier typography

Invitation display is separated from compact purchase data; larger image-led compositions benefit from a clear typographic anchor. Quiet lacks authorship, ledger overweights price.

Georgia is used only for invitation, section headings and large prices; OS sans carries instructions, terms and controls. Display 48–112px responsive, line height 1.03–1.12, tracking no tighter than -.035em; headings 32–72px; body 16px/1.65 with 52–65ch measures. Controls at least 44px tall. Numeric totals use tabular figures. Indonesian and Latin punctuation use platform fonts. No font downloads or licenses for redistributed binaries. Fallback Times New Roman/serif and system sans. Three same-copy monochrome specimens in docs/atelier.
