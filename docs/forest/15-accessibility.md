# Stage 15 — accessibility and usable editorial reading

## Baseline findings and corrections

Real Chrome / axe-core WCAG 2.x + best-practice testing of both compiled GitHub Pages routes initially identified two recurring violation classes: insufficient text contrast and nested complementary landmarks. The baseline audit failed before remediation.

Production changes preserve the locked Forest V2 art direction while improving: contrast of Work marginalia, Path and River numbered annotations, and the House closing eyebrow; distinct visible keyboard focus against both paper and deep forest; accessibility of field-register controls; forced-colors and increased-contrast presentation; correct semantics for inline notes and the House inquiry section; and native skip, chapter, note and folio fragment destinations made focusable via tabindex=-1 without adding extra Tab stops.

QA uses Chrome and axe-core on both built routes and multiple viewports, plus native keyboard, menu Escape/focus, no-JS access, reduced motion and no overflow. No browser screenshots, screen recordings or videos are captured or uploaded. Automated testing does not prove full WCAG conformance or substitute for manual screen-reader testing.

## Editorial quality still unresolved

An automated accessibility pass cannot certify that this illustrated website feels richly art-directed or non-generic. Stage 17 must review visual quality, composition, texture, typographic hierarchy, chapter storytelling and the substance of studio content. Stage 16 separately concerns performance. The House note remains a local-only draft, not a connected lead-delivery form.
