## Acceptance method

1. Scale the vector sheet down to a postcard; cover all panel labels.
2. Ask a reader to identify clearing, river, house from silhouette alone.
3. Verify that strong gold is **reserved** for the Atrium door and house window; river stays cooler.
4. In real finished portraits, align the Paperkeeper's feet to the original physical ground line, not to the SVG diagrams.
5. Render and compare a final production painting pair at each chapter size. The schematic sheets do not replace that editorial approval.

## Implemented continuity, not inflated claims

The entered Atrium and its movable light are first-pass interactive 2D illustrations. The river prop and house window repeat the *same* torn-corner SVG geometry; the existing Paperkeeper raster sprite remains canonical. Finite character acting uses poses that exist rather than claiming newly commissioned hand-drawn frames. The house remains a local-only Field Letter editor, not a contact-form submission.

Physical one-handed device and human art acceptance remain outside automated CI.
