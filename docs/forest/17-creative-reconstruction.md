# Stage 17 — Creative editorial reconstruction, not a decorative audit

## Evidence from the checked-in source

The five scenes already contained illustration and functional components, but the visible website relied heavily on repeating two-column chapter introductions and three **concept-only** Work folios. That structure made it too easy for a visitor to read the forest as a decorative landing page rather than a connected editorial experience. The earlier reported user concern that the site still feels basic is therefore treated as an active design issue, not contradicted by green CI.

This review is limited to source, semantic markup, CSS, asset provenance and browser DOM/computed styles: **no automatic visual screenshots or recordings** are permitted. It is not a human aesthetic sign-off.

## Two implemented responses

### 01 / A fold-out Field Atlas

The Entrance contains a fully native `<details>` paper insert, with a typographic chapter index and five original wayfinding destinations. Its map is **editorial cartography**: hand-directed SVG annotations of the already-authored portal, roots, hanging sheets, river and cottage. It is not a replacement for any hero landscape artwork and not a fabricated physical hand-drawn original. The index exposes native anchors; the map is decorative. Five landmarks use Roman glyphs, pencil strokes, and constrained colors from the locked forest. Opening is voluntary, no page scroll is intercepted, and all contents are reachable without JavaScript.

The insert is deliberately an **atlas you can unfold**, not a generic floating dot navigator, popup, animated overlay, card-grid or progress bar. On mobile the two bound leaves stack with their typography intact.

### 02 / A real studio specimen in Work Clearing

The three original folios retain their honest labels as **concept studies, not commissioned work**. A different paper sheet now presents **this very website** as a verifiable studio experiment. Three observations address Paperkeeper's character, visitor-controlled motion, and the book-like material and navigation. Every observation points to a genuine chapter. No client, testimonial, award, time-saving number or fabricated deployment success is invented. Unlike hypothetical concepts, this is a demonstrable piece of actual work inside the current page.

The material language uses cut paper, a double border, a slightly irregular ink seal and page-edge details; paragraphs remain normal selectable HTML. Type is editorial Fraunces/Newsreader/Atkinson, not text baked into a hero image. There are no external libraries, extra runtime scripts, fake pencil-grain textures or network assets.

## Critical art-direction honesty

The code changes improve one point of narrative continuity and one content-credibility gap; **they do not prove that every raster painting, mobile SVG, character silhouette, stroke quality or chapter composition now meets the user-approved standard**. In particular, Stage 14 portrait art uses a different scripted-SVG technique from desktop raster paintings, and the source-only review cannot certify their aesthetic match.

The lock file demands human inspection and per-criterion ratings of at least 4/5 with mean at least 4.5/5 before sign-off. These ratings are **not asserted** here. This release is a concrete creative improvement, with final aesthetic acceptance pending user-directed visual review; no screenshots have been captured by the agent. The local Field Letter still does not send inquiries without a verified studio contact channel.

Stage 18 launch is not started by this step.
